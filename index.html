Grand Theft Auto VI arrives on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026. The download starts earlier. Digital preload opens on November 10, and the storefront install size is about 212 GB. Waiting until that morning to hunt for free space means deleting games while the store is at its busiest.

This is the practical version of the job: how much to clear, what to remove first, and why the two consoles do not open preload at the same moment. The wider desk lives on the HUDWire GTA 6 page. Use the checklist below on the console that will actually run the game.

The dates that change your evening

  • Launch is November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
  • Digital preload starts November 10, 2026.
  • On PS5, that start is midnight in your local time zone.
  • On Xbox Series X|S, preload starts at 12:00 a.m. GMT on November 10. In the Americas that is the evening of November 9: 7:00 p.m. Eastern and 4:00 p.m. Pacific. In central Europe it is 1:00 a.m. local time.
  • Boxed editions are a download code in the case. Retail copies of that code start arriving November 12, so a code-in-box buyer has a shorter preload window than an account that already owns a digital license.
  • Preload only copies files. It does not unlock the game before the regional launch time.

Rockstar’s current Grand Theft Auto VI product page lists the November 19 release for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It does not list a PC version for day one. There is no PC preload to join and no PC file size to reserve yet.

The hour-by-hour windows, including that Xbox GMT start, are written up separately in GTA 6 preload time.

How much free space to aim for

Treat about 212 GB as the storefront install size, with the caveat Rockstar actually published: the preload file needs up to 212 GB depending on the device. One console may land under that ceiling. Plan for the ceiling.

Keep roughly 230–250 GB free on the drive the game will install to, before you start the download. The extra 20–40 GB is not a second copy of the game. It covers three ordinary console behaviors:

  1. A large download often needs a little working room beyond the final install size. If the only free block you have is 212 GB exactly, the install can fail near the end and leave you deleting files at the worst moment.
  2. Launch week can add a day-one update on top of a preload that finished days earlier.
  3. The system still needs space for its own data, captures, and whatever else you refuse to uninstall.

The gigabyte figure will move around store listings as patches land. GTA 6 file size is where that number is being tracked. The 230–250 GB target is a HUDWire planning number, not a second official size.

What to clear first

Tick these in order and stop once internal free space clears 230 GB. On a 1 TB drive, 250 GB is the more comfortable mark. External USB space does not count toward the number you need in order to play.

PlayStation 5

Open Settings → Storage → Console Storage. Games and Apps is the large pool. Captures sit under Media, or under the same storage screen’s media category, and they are the fastest clean win because nobody is mid-campaign in a clip.

Sony’s original 825 GB PS5 leaves roughly 667 GB for games and apps, so a 212 GB install is about a third of a base console before anything else you own. Slim and later 1 TB models have more room. An internal M.2 expansion drive is the clean way to add space the PS5 can play from. USB extended storage can hold PS4 games and parked PS5 installs; plan on moving a PS5-format game back to internal storage before you play it.

Digital preload on PS5 begins at midnight local time on November 10. If you are buying the code in a box instead, you cannot start that download until the code is redeemed, and those retail codes begin showing up on November 12.

Xbox Series X|S

Use Settings → System → Storage, or My games & apps → Manage → Free up space. The manage screen is the one that sorts by size, which is what you want when you are trying to free a quarter of a terabyte in one sitting.

The 512 GB Series S is the console this launch is hardest on. After the system reservation, usable space on that model is on the order of 360 GB, and a preload approaching 212 GB can leave room for the operating system, captures, and very little else. A 1 TB Series X, with roughly 800 GB usable, or a 1 TB Series S, can take the install and still keep a short library. The official storage expansion card counts as internal space. A USB hard drive does not, for playing Series X|S titles.

Do not wait for local midnight if you are on Xbox. Preload opens at 12:00 a.m. GMT on November 10 for every region at once. On the US East Coast that is 7:00 p.m. on November 9. Starting then, instead of at local midnight, is most of a night of download time you do not have to spend on launch day.

The night preload opens

  1. Confirm the license is on the account that will play it. Family sharing only helps if this console is already allowed to download that license.
  2. Use Ethernet if you can, or Wi-Fi you are allowed to saturate. At about 212 GB, even a solid home connection is a long evening. A slow one is an overnight job that can spill into the next day.
  3. Start the download from the game’s store page or library once the window opens for your platform. There is no separate preload code.
  4. Leave the console awake or in rest mode with network downloads enabled if you want the file to finish while you sleep. Turn off a hard shutdown timer if your console pauses downloads when it powers down.
  5. If the install stops on a storage error, delete more and resume. You do not need to buy the game again.
  6. You still cannot play until launch on November 19.

After the files are on the drive

Check the installed size once, then leave it. On the morning of November 19, look at free space again before you sit down. If a day-one patch is offered, let it finish before you load in. An interrupted update is a worse start than a short wait, and it is the reason the 230–250 GB cushion exists.

PC players sitting this launch out can skip the cleanup. Until Rockstar’s product page lists a PC edition and a date, there is nothing to preload. Console notes, the preload clock, and the 212 GB file-size watch stay on HUDWire.
